Electronic Waste (E-Waste) Management Guide

Electronic waste, including discarded computers, circuit boards, display screens, and cabling arrays, is characterized by its high structural complexity. While containing precious metals, e-waste also contains hazardous chemicals like lead, cadmium, and mercury that require safe handling processes.

Primary E-Waste Assets

Recycling networks divide electronic items into distinct scrap groups:

  • Printed Circuit Boards (PCBs): High-density cards from computers, routers, and mobile devices containing copper, gold, and silver trace alloys.
  • IT Core Hardware: Desktop motherboards, server enclosures, microprocessors, RAM components, and storage units.
  • Display Panels: Obsolete computer screens, monitors, and television sets.
  • Power Accessories: Insulated copper power cables, UPS batteries, and converter units.

Safe Disposal Guidelines

To manage e-waste responsibly in Jaipur:

  1. Avoid breaking screens or monitor panels manually, which can release mercury vapors or hazardous phosphor coatings.
  2. Do not burn insulated copper cables. Incineration releases toxic dioxins into the atmosphere.
  3. Store old backup batteries in dry, cool conditions to prevent acid leakage before pickup.
