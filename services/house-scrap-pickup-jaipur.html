Residential & House Scrap Collection Guide

Domestic households regularly generate dry recyclables that can be diverted from Municipal Solid Waste streams. Correct segregation at the household level ensures materials retain their processing value. In Vaishali Nagar and across Jaipur, homeowners can arrange structured collection runs to dispose of accumulated items systematically.

Typical Household Scrap Classifications

When sorting household scrap, items are generally grouped into the following categories:

  • Packaging Cardboard & Paper: Corrugated boxes, packaging inserts, books, textbooks, office stationery, and newsprint bundles.
  • Plastic Materials: Obsolete plastic buckets, soft drink bottles, large containers, and toys.
  • Household Metals: Iron window grilles, old metal utensils, copper wire segments, aluminum containers, and brass valves.
  • Defunct Home Appliances: Broken washing machines, inactive refrigerators, old microwave casings, and mixer grinders.

Pre-collection Sorting Best Practices

Before arranging a collection, residents are encouraged to:

  1. Keep paper dry. Wet paper cannot be pulped effectively at recycling mills in Jaipur.
  2. Empty and rinse plastic containers to ensure cleanliness.
  3. Bundle corrugated boxes together using twine for fast transport.
