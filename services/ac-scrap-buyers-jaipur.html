Air Conditioner Scrap & Cooling System Disposal

Obsolete or damaged cooling units, such as window ACs, split AC outdoor units, and centralized chiller assemblies, contain high-value recyclable components, specifically copper and aluminum. However, because they contain chemical refrigerants, their decommissioning must follow safe environmental standards.

Key Recyclable Elements in AC Units

During the dismantling process, sorting centers extract specific materials:

  • Copper Tubing & Coils: Located in the condenser and evaporator blocks. This represents the highest value material in the assembly.
  • Hermetic Compressors: Heavy steel shells enclosing electrical motor windings and piston systems.
  • Aluminum Fins: Thin plates surrounding the copper cooling lines to facilitate heat exchange.
  • Sheet Metal Casings: The external protective frame of the appliance.

Safe Decommissioning Steps

To prevent environmental contamination in Jaipur:

  1. Ensure the unit is completely disconnected from power supplies before handling.
  2. Avoid cutting refrigerant gas lines manually, which can release ozone-depleting gases into the atmosphere.
  3. Request professional dismantling if the outdoor unit is mounted on high external brackets.
