Old Furniture Scrap Disposal Guide

Old or broken furniture units—including steel-framed cabinets, solid wood tables, modular shelves, and office desks—represent bulky waste elements that require structured dismantling. When furniture is no longer reusable, recovering its basic materials (wood, iron, plastic) contributes to local circular recycling runs in Jaipur.

Key Furniture Scrap Categories

Processing centers evaluate furniture units based on their primary structural materials:

  • Metal-Framed Furniture: Steel wardrobes, iron beds, filing drawers, and modular computer desk frameworks. These contain high-value recyclable steel.
  • Wooden Elements: Solid timber doors, broken plywood cabinets, and panels. Non-treated wood is routed to chipboard mills.
  • Hybrid Assemblies: Sofas, upholstered chairs, and partition panels. The fabric, cushions, and foam must be separated from structural frames before recycling.

Pre-pickup Dismantling Best Practices

To facilitate transport and material recovery:

  1. Dismantle drawer units and unscrew steel legs to make transport easier.
  2. Remove mirrors or glass panes from wooden doors. Glass requires completely separate processing pathways.
  3. Separate metal bolts or plastic attachments from wooden panels where possible.
