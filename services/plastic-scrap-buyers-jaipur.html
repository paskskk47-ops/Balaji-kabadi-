Plastic Scrap Sorting & Recycling Guidelines

Unlike metals, plastic structures are chemically diverse. Standard sorting based on resin codes is critical, as mixing incompatible polymers degrades the quality of reformed plastic materials. Diverting plastic containers from local street drains protects urban ecosystems in Jaipur.

Common Polymer Classifications

Recycling systems sort plastics based on their polymer structure:

  • PET (Polyethylene Terephthalate): Soft drink bottles, water bottles, and thin packaging containers.
  • HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene): Thick milk jars, water pipes, chemical containers, and structural drums.
  • PP (Polypropylene): Food containers, broken chairs, durable plastic crates, and furniture structures.
  • PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride): Electrical cable insulation conduit, water drainage pipes, and structural panel profiles.

Pre-collection Sorting Rules

To prepare plastic items for recycling:

  1. Wash containers to eliminate organic food residue or chemicals.
  2. Group thick plastics (like broken PP chairs) separately from thin film bags.
  3. Separate metal caps or screws from plastic jars.

Local Collection Networks

After segregation, items are transported to local processing facilities. Local logistics partners, such as Balaji Best Kabadi Wala, collect separated plastics from domestic and commercial properties in Vaishali Nagar, routing them to industrial shredding centers in Rajasthan.
