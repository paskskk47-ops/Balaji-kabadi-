How to Sell Scrap & Dry Recyclables: Step-by-Step

Selling dry household or commercial recyclables is an effective way to clean your space while helping the local circular economy. Following a systematic sorting and scheduling process ensures your items are measured accurately and sent to appropriate processing centers.

Step 1: Sorting and Segregation

Before arranging a collection run, group items into separate piles based on material type:

  • Paper Products: Keep newspaper bundles, cardboard sheets, and books together. Make sure they are stored in a dry place.
  • Plastics: Empty any bottles or containers. Group heavy polypropylene items (like broken chairs) away from soft wrapping films.
  • Scrap Metals: Ferrous iron grilles or pipes should be kept separate from non-ferrous copper wires or brass fittings.

Step 2: Checking Weight Accuracy

Correct measurement is critical in scrap selling. When sorting at your doorstep:

  1. Ensure the scale used is digital and calibrated correctly.
  2. Weigh heavy items in distinct groups rather than mixing wood, plastic, and metal together.
  3. Check that the scale displays zero before items are loaded for weight verification.

Step 3: Scheduling Doorstep Pickup

Rather than transport heavy metal grilles or old washing machines yourself, schedule a doorstep run with an established operator.
