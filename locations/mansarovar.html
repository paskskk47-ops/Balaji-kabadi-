Mansarovar Scrap & Recycling Guide

Mansarovar, recognized as one of Asia's largest residential colonies, is a massive urban sprawl in the southern corridor of Jaipur. The area contains a high volume of single-family housing estates, coaching institutes, schools, shopping complexes, and retail markets. Working with verified Scrap Buyers in Jaipur helps residents clear out packaging materials, old study guides, student hostel household waste, and office assets.

Key Focus Zones in Mansarovar

Waste sorting and collection logistics support several distinct sectors within Mansarovar:

  • Madhyam Marg: The principal commercial hub of Mansarovar, featuring shopping blocks, corporate branches, and retail showrooms generating packing cardboards and packaging sheets.
  • Mansarovar Plaza & VT Road: Bustling areas with retail offices and commercial hubs requiring scheduled electronics and office furniture collections.

Sorting and Recycling Protocols

Because Mansarovar generates highly diverse waste streams, local guidelines emphasize sorting categories:
