Nirman Nagar Scrap & Dry Waste Sorting Guide

Nirman Nagar is a well-established residential area in western Jaipur, situated near the DCM commercial junction. Flanked by Ranisati Nagar and surrounding housing complexes, Nirman Nagar features many single-family properties, schools, parks, and residential roads, generating cardboard boxes, household packaging plastics, metal scrap, and obsolete utility appliances.

Key Focus Zones in Nirman Nagar

Waste collection runs support several key sectors within the Nirman Nagar area:

  • DCM Border Corridor: A major commercial connection point where local retail units require packaging cardboard and steel scrap clearing.
  • Residential Blocks (A to E): Wide residential roads where family homes regularly sort old newspapers, books, and household plastics.
  • Nirman Nagar Community Parks: Focus zones for neighborhood associations to organize community cleanups and sorting runs.

Recommended Scrap Sorting Categories

To maintain environmental standards:

  1. White Paper & Cardboard: Stack old textbooks, textbooks, newsprint, and delivery boxes in dry conditions.
  2. HDPE & PP Plastics: Obsolete plastic drums, buckets, chairs, and household packaging containers.
  3. Household Metals: Iron grilles from home extensions, copper lines, and old steel fixtures.
