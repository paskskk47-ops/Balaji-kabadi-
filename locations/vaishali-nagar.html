Pre-Collection Sorting Best Practices

To prepare scrap items for collection and optimize processing efficiency:

  • Segregate Paper: Store books and cardboard boxes in dry areas. Damp paper cannot be processed effectively at local paper pulp mills.
  • Separate Plastics from Metals: Remove plastic handles, wheels, or casings from heavy metal frames where possible.
  • Organize Wood Waste: Keep furniture separate. Working with professional Wood Scrap Buyers in Vaishali Nagar helps redirect clean timber to industrial board manufacturers.
